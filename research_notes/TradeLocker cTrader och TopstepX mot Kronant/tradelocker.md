## Q1. What features does the TradeLocker terminal offer?

### Takeaway
TradeLocker is a chart-first discretionary terminal: native TradingView charts with on-chart trading, a strong risk/position-size calculator (SL/TP in $, %, ticks or price), trailing stop, break-even button, one-click trading, web + Windows desktop + iOS/Android, 9+ languages, an AI no-code bot builder (Studio) and a public API. It notably lacks native trade journaling, any CSV/PDF history export, native copy trading and full white-label branding; multi-chart layouts (max 4) and a unified multi-account login only arrived in mid-2026.

### Cited Findings
Charting
- Native TradingView charting with indicator library, drawing tools, chart templates and multiple timeframes, including 2, 3 and 10-minute charts that TradingView normally locks behind paid plans (snippet) - [proptradingvibes TradeLocker review 2026](https://proptradingvibes.com/blog/tradelocker-review)
- "100+ TradingView indicators integrated directly"; draggable red/green SL/TP lines; risk-reward overlay zones on chart; customizable candle colours, backgrounds, grid - [damnpropfirms, 2026-04-09, updated 2026-07](https://damnpropfirms.com/trading-guides/tradelocker-review-2026-tradingview-powered-platform/)
- Chart performance boost and history on 1-second, 15-second and 30-second timeframes (2025-05-26) - [TradeLocker newsroom: features](https://tradelocker.com/news-category/features/)
- Multiple chart layout: "up to 4 independent charts" with own symbols, timeframes and indicators on all devices (2026-06-01). Before that, "Multiple charts" was a roadmap item - [TradeLocker newsroom](https://tradelocker.com/news-category/features/); [TradeLocker roadmap](https://tradelocker.com/roadmap/)
- Light/dark themes released - [TradeLocker roadmap](https://tradelocker.com/roadmap/)

Order entry, SL/TP, risk
- Order types: market, limit, stop, pending orders, SL/TP, partial close, one-click execution, micro lots (0.01); "order panel is streamlined compared to MetaTrader's cluttered interface"; position grouping - [New York City Servers review, 2026-02-19](https://newyorkcityservers.com/blog/tradelocker-review)
- "SL&TP Calculator": traders "visually determine their risk tolerance" and "Calculate with $, %, Ticks and Price parameters"; trailing stop loss; "Margin call & stop out" with visual margin level bar - [TradeLocker prop firm trading platform page](https://tradelocker.com/prop-firm-trading-platform/)
- Risk-based position sizing from account balance and risk %, shows exact P&L at target/stop before the trade is placed; price alerts (snippet) - [TradeLocker best platforms page](https://tradelocker.com/forex-trading/best-forex-trading-platforms/)
- One-tap Break Even (move SL/TP to entry) launched 2025-06-12; order amount can be set "in your currency" instead of lots (2025-06-03) - [TradeLocker newsroom](https://tradelocker.com/news-category/features/)

Copy trading, algo, API, social
- Official: TradeLocker "does not currently offer direct copy trading as a platform feature"; it points to partner copiers and will not troubleshoot third-party copiers (snippet of support article) - [TradeLocker support: Copy trading](https://support.tradelocker.com/en/articles/13672956-copy-trading). - Public REST API with per-route rate limits exposed via a /config endpoint; a "Developer Program" unlocks less restrictive limits for multi-account/multi-user apps; official Python client; Studio ships inside the Desktop app (snippet) - [TradeLocker public API docs](https://public-api.tradelocker.com); [PyPI tradelocker](https://pypi.org/project/tradelocker/)
- No MQL/EA support; "Less mature automation than MQL or cAlgo" - [NYC Servers review](https://newyorkcityservers.com/blog/tradelocker-review)
- Discord is used for feature requests ("Every submission will be reviewed by our team"); "Hub Bots" marketplace and "social mode" are roadmap items - [TradeLocker roadmap](https://tradelocker.com/roadmap/)
- TradeLocker Hub (launched 2025-01-29): in-platform marketplace listing brokers, prop firms, bots and copy-trading options - [TradeLocker newsroom](https://tradelocker.com/news-category/features/); [TradeLocker Hub](https://tradelocker.com/hub)

### Inferences
- The risk calculator ($/%/ticks/price) with P&L preview, draggable SL/TP and the one-tap break-even are the features most worth copying; they map directly onto prop-trader needs (fixed % risk per trade).
- Missing native journal, CSV/PDF export and copy trading are clear, cheap-to-close gaps for Kronant Trader, especially since Kronant owns the back office and can produce firm-branded statements directly.
- Multi-chart (4) and a unified multi-account profile were only added in May-June 2026, so a Kronant terminal that ships multi-chart and challenge/funded account switching from day one is at parity, not ahead.

### Gaps
- Exact list of order types (e.g. OCO, stop-limit, order expiry/GTD) not confirmed from an official spec; one Trustpilot review (Jul 2026) says pending orders lack expiration dates (see Q4).
- Whether hedging (opposite positions on one symbol) is supported is not documented in the sources found; it depends on broker/prop configuration.
- Current full language list after April 2025 not found.
- Whether a macOS desktop app exists was not confirmed (only Windows seen).

## Q2. How does TradeLocker show prop challenge rules (drawdown, daily loss, targets) inside the terminal?

### Takeaway
No evidence was found that the TradeLocker terminal shows challenge objectives (daily loss left, max drawdown, profit target, trading days) to the trader. TradeLocker's own help center says it has no access to breach logic at all; rule evaluation and the objective dashboards live in each prop firm's own back office/CRM.

### Cited Findings
- Official: "TradeLocker cannot review or reverse challenge results. TradeLocker does not have access to breach logic or evaluation rules." "Only your prop firm has access to your full trading evaluation and rule enforcement." - [TradeLocker support: Breached account or challenge in drawdown](https://support.tradelocker.com/en/articles/13671916-breached-account-or-challenge-in-drawdown)
- Official prop firm page lists margin level bar, SL/TP calculator, trailing stop, portfolio trackers, CRM connection and "Compliance reporting and auditing", but no challenge-rule engine or objectives panel - [TradeLocker prop firm trading platform](https://tradelocker.com/prop-firm-trading-platform/)
- Official prop pricing: "Back Office admin tools", "Advanced reporting", "CRM API connection"; no rule engine listed - [TradeLocker prop firm pricing](https://tradelocker.com/prop-firm-pricing/)
- Earlier pricing research in this repo concluded "TradeLocker sells the platform only (CRM via API)" - see research_notes/Konkurrenters priser och vårt pris/all_in_one_platforms.md (sourced to [TradeLocker prop firm pricing](https://tradelocker.com/prop-firm-pricing/))
- Rules are surfaced only as listing data: Hub pages show e.g. FTUK 5% daily drawdown, FunderPro 5% max daily loss, ThePropTrade 3% balance-based daily loss (snippet) - [TradeLocker Hub: FTUK](https://tradelocker.com/hub/prop-firms/ftuk); [FunderPro](https://tradelocker.com/hub/prop-firms/funderpro); [ThePropTrade](https://tradelocker.com/hub/prop-firms/theproptrade)
- Two independent 2026 reviews explicitly do not mention any in-terminal rule display - [damnpropfirms 2026](https://damnpropfirms.com/trading-guides/tradelocker-review-2026-tradingview-powered-platform/); [NYC Servers 2026](https://newyorkcityservers.com/blog/tradelocker-review)

### Inferences
- This is the biggest structural opportunity for Kronant: because Kronant Prop and Kronant Trader are one stack, the terminal can show live "daily loss left / max drawdown left / target progress" next to the order ticket and even pre-trade warnings ("this order at SL would breach daily loss"). TradeLocker architecturally cannot do this without each firm's CRM feeding data back.
- TradeLocker's support repeatedly redirecting traders to "your prop firm" (breaches, unclosable trades, SL execution) is a UX seam traders feel; a single-vendor stack can give one coherent answer.

### Gaps
- Could not verify whether any prop firm injects a rules widget into TradeLocker via an embedded view or custom build; no source found either way.

## Q3. What do traders praise most?

### Takeaway
Praise is consistent across Trustpilot, the App Store and reviews: clean, modern, easy UI ("better than MT4/MT5"), TradingView charts without a TradingView subscription, and the built-in risk/position-size tools. Ratings are high (Trustpilot 4.5 from ~2,250 reviews; iOS 4.4 from ~2.8K ratings), although some reviewers allege review manipulation.

### Cited Findings
- Trustpilot: TrustScore 4.5, 2,250 reviews, 249 in last 12 months; 78% 5-star, 12% 4-star, 2% 3-star, 1% 2-star, 7% 1-star. Praise themes: user-friendly/intuitive UI, comparisons favouring it over MT4/MT5, SL/TP and risk calculators, fast execution, TradingView charts. Example titles: "SO MUCH BETTER THAN MT4 or MT5"; "Fantastic Platform, specially for risk management" (2025-12-04); "easy to navigate user friendly" (2026-09-08). Company replies to 50% of negative reviews, usually within 2 weeks (fetched 2026-10-06) - [Trustpilot TradeLocker](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tradelocker.com)
- iOS App Store (US): 4.4 stars, 2.8K ratings; reviews praise execution speed, clean design, TradingView integration and auto position sizing - [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tradelocker/id6447196449)
- Aggregate across iOS and Android: 4.37 from 6,278 reviews (snippet, aggregator) - [AppFollow](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/tradelocker/6447196449?country=in)
- Review-site pros: native TradingView integration "eliminates $15-$60 monthly subscription costs", synced cloud layouts, full mobile charting and order management, AI bot creation for non-coders - [damnpropfirms 2026](https://damnpropfirms.com/trading-guides/tradelocker-review-2026-tradingview-powered-platform/); "Clean, modern interface with low learning curve", "Strong mobile app experience" - [NYC Servers 2026](https://newyorkcityservers.com/blog/tradelocker-review)
- Positioning by comparison sites: best for "chart-first discretionary" traders who value "built-in risk sizing ... in a prop-style environment where risk rules punish small mistakes" (snippet; this is TradeLocker's own comparison page, so self-promotional) - [TradeLocker vs cTrader](https://tradelocker.com/forex-trading/tradelocker-vs-ctrader/)
- Third-party: "Modern UI", "TradingView-powered platform with native prop firm integration", web-first, no download required - [PropFirmMap, 2026-04-08](https://propfirmmap.com/blog/prop-firm-platforms-mt5-ctrader-match-trader-compared-2026)

### Inferences
- Traders reward simplicity plus risk tooling more than feature depth. Kronant's terminal should keep the order ticket minimal while making %-risk sizing the default.
- High star ratings coexist with severe 1-star outage reviews: average sentiment is good, but tail events (outages during live trading) drive the angry reviews and the prop firm churn (see Q4, Q5).

- Reddit (r/Forex, r/propfirm, r/Daytrading), Forex Factory and YouTube sentiment could not be accessed; frequency of praise there is unknown.
- Google Play rating and review count could not be fetched (page returned truncated content).

## Q4. What do traders complain about most? (incidents with dates)

### Takeaway
Main complaint clusters: (1) outages/freezes during active sessions, with two press-reported >1 hour outages (DDoS 2024-04-17; Cloudflare-related 2026-07-30); (2) fills/slippage and closing-price mismatches, especially on indices at the NY open; (3) settings/favourites/templates not syncing across devices; (4) missing features (close-all, longer history, EAs, export, order expiry); (5) confusion about who is responsible (TradeLocker vs broker/prop firm). Complaints are a small share of all reviews (about 10% of Trustpilot reviews are 1-3 stars) but are severe.

### Cited Findings
Incidents
- 2024-04-17 (older): DDoS attack, downtime 13:12-14:25 UTC per Funding Pips; affected Funding Pips, Tradddo, Funding Traders, TopTier Trader. Funding Pips' CEO announced it on Discord/X the same day; TradeLocker only confirmed on 2024-04-18. Funding Pips said it would "rectify impacted accounts" using equity levels before/after the outage, as there was no transaction data during the downtime; TradeLocker announced extra security measures - [Finance Magnates, 2024-04-18](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/funding-pips-claims-tradelocker-fell-victim-to-ddos-platform-confirms-only-a-day-later/)
- 2026-07-30: platform inaccessible for many users for over an hour, apparently a Cloudflare problem; TradeLocker's status page gave no cause; prop firms had to explain it (Whales Capital Funding: "We are currently experiencing a technical issue with TradeLocker"); firms assured traders that pending orders, TP and SL would still execute - [TradeInformer](https://tradeinformer.com/tech-news/tradelocker-bug-shuts-traders-out-for-over-an-hour)
- Corroboration: four 1-star Trustpilot reviews dated 2026-07-30 ("Horrible" - outages during high-volume times and lockout during trades; server problems preventing closing trades or modifying SL/TP; app freezes in NY session; "Often shuts down, logs me out mid trade") - [Trustpilot 1-star filter](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tradelocker.com?stars=1)
- A third-party status tracker monitors the TradeLocker API via Traders Connect (no incident history read) - [StatusGator](https://statusgator.com/services/traders-connect/tradelocker-api--live)

Execution and fills
- "fills on indices are terrible between 930 and 10" (2026-09-10); "fills can be unreliable ... clunky inaccurate platform" (2026-09-28); closing price discrepancy (2026-09-01) - [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tradelocker.com); [Trustpilot 1-star](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tradelocker.com?stars=1)
- Execution timing claim: ~0.4 s normal, ~2.4 s in high volatility, "Not ideal" for sub-1-minute scalping - [NYC Servers 2026](https://newyorkcityservers.com/blog/tradelocker-review); same figures repeated in [damnpropfirms 2026](https://damnpropfirms.com/trading-guides/tradelocker-review-2026-tradingview-powered-platform/). Methodology not disclosed; NYC Servers sells VPS hosting, so treat as unverified. Contradicted in tone by another third-party test claiming "tens of milliseconds" execution on a low-latency host and news slippage comparable to cTrader (snippet; the search summary did not clearly attribute it, most likely the Goat Funded Trader blog, a firm that sells TradeLocker accounts) - [Goat Funded Trader blog](https://www.goatfundedtrader.com/blog/tradelocker-prop-firm)
- Note: execution quality is largely set by the broker/prop firm's server or TradeLocker's SLE, so fill complaints partly reflect individual firms.

Sync, UX and missing features
- "my favorites do not save if I sign in on different computer" (2026-09-03); lack of customizable templates; lag during peak hours - [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tradelocker.com)
- "custom templates reset when switching devices" - [NYC Servers 2026](https://newyorkcityservers.com/blog/tradelocker-review); [damnpropfirms 2026](https://damnpropfirms.com/trading-guides/tradelocker-review-2026-tradingview-powered-platform/)
- App Store complaints: SL/TP set in "draft" mode did not execute and price passed through (July 2025; developer replied that a confirmation step is required, which users found counterintuitive); unexpected auto-closes attributed to stop-outs; requests for a simpler beginner UI, "close all trades", longer chart history, EA support - [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tradelocker/id6447196449)
- "Just stick to MT5": no mobile app in reviewer's region, unclear pending-order interface, no order expiration dates (2026-07-11) - [Trustpilot 1-star](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tradelocker.com?stars=1)
- No CSV/PDF export or journal (official) - [TradeLocker support](https://support.tradelocker.com/en/articles/13752344-journaling-and-history-download)
- "basic risk features" compared with Match-Trader (snippet, competitor-adjacent comparison blog) - [pipstonecapital](https://pipstonecapital.com/blogs/match-trader-vs-c-trader-vs-tradelocker)

Responsibility and reputation
- Official help: when a close/edit fails "the broker's server is most likely rejecting the request"; TradeLocker "cannot force close positions or override broker decisions" - [TradeLocker support: Can not close or edit trades](https://support.tradelocker.com/en/articles/14121180-can-not-close-or-edit-trades)
- Trustpilot reply: stop losses "are managed by the broker or prop firm ... not by TradeLocker directly" - [Trustpilot 1-star](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tradelocker.com?stars=1)
- Reputation spillover: 1-star reviews (Sep 2026) accuse TradeLocker of supporting a fraudulent prop firm (Plutus Trade Base) and allege fake positive reviews; 2026-06-20 review alleges trade records not matching (lot sizes, pairs). These are unverified allegations - [Trustpilot 1-star](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tradelocker.com?stars=1)

Rough frequency
- Trustpilot overall: 7% 1-star + 1% 2-star + 2% 3-star = about 10% negative/neutral - [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tradelocker.com)
- Of 11 visible 1-star reviews (2026-06-20 to 2026-09-28): 5 outage/freeze/logout, 3 slippage/close-price, 2 prop-firm/fraud allegations, 1 support non-response - [Trustpilot 1-star](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tradelocker.com?stars=1)

### Inferences
- The most damaging failure mode is "cannot close/modify during an outage while a challenge is live". Kronant should treat server-side SL/TP persistence, a public status page with cause statements, and an automatic, documented outage-compensation workflow in the back office (e.g. freeze breaches during a declared incident) as product features, not ops afterthoughts.
- Cross-device persistence of favourites, templates and layouts is a repeated, cheap-to-fix irritation; store all terminal preferences server-side per user.
- Explicit order confirmation steps (the "draft SL/TP" problem) must be visually obvious; an unconfirmed SL should look clearly different from an active one.
- Being a vendor that sits between traders and firms creates blame-shifting; Kronant's integrated model can show the trader exactly why an order was rejected (rule, margin, market closed).

### Gaps
- No Reddit/Forex Factory incident threads could be read, so less-publicised outages (2025) may be missing.
- No public TradeLocker status-page incident history was retrieved.
- No hard data on requote/rejection rates.

## Q5. Which prop firms use it, why did they choose it after the 2024 MetaQuotes crackdown, and what do firms say about the backend?

### Takeaway
TradeLocker won early prop adoption in Feb-July 2024 (Top Tier Trader, Funding Pips, Goat Funded Trader, Lionheart) as a fast-to-launch, modern MT4/MT5 replacement. In 2026 it is a second-tier platform by prop adoption (about 15% of 53 tracked firms vs MT5 43%, Match-Trader 30%, cTrader 26%), with 26 prop firms on its Hub, at least one firm (Funded Squad) dropping it for reliability, several firms bringing MT5 back, and its broker list shrinking.

### Cited Findings
Who uses it
- TradeLocker Hub lists "1 - 26 of 26" prop firms, visible ones: OneFunded, Blue Guardian, Clarity Traders, FunderPro, Atlas Funded, Top One Trader, Alpha Trader, FTUK, Eightcap Challenges, OFP Funding (fetched 2026-10-06) - [TradeLocker Hub prop firms](https://tradelocker.com/hub/prop-firms)
- PropFirmMap (2026-04-08, 53 active firms): MT5 43% (23), Match-Trader 30% (16), cTrader 26% (14), DXtrade 19% (10), TradeLocker 15% (8): Goat Funded Trader, Blue Guardian, Hola Prime, Atlas Funded, Funding Traders, For Traders, DNA Funded, Alpha Capital Group - [PropFirmMap](https://propfirmmap.com/blog/prop-firm-platforms-mt5-ctrader-match-trader-compared-2026)
- "more than 60 partner brokers and prop firms", including Alpha Capital Group, Blueberry Funded, Top One Trader; brokers Eightcap, GatesFX, HeroFX - [Finance Magnates, 2026-05-13](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/products/tradelocker-opens-its-demo-to-all-users-joining-a-path-already-cleared-by-ctrader-and-metaquotes/)

## Q6. What is its pricing/business model toward brokers and prop firms?

### Takeaway
Public SaaS list prices: prop firms USD 3,000/month Starter covering up to 500 active accounts (billed on accounts that actually trade, minimum fee applies, no setup fee), custom tiered pricing above 500; brokers USD 5,000/month for up to 1,000 live and 2,000 demo accounts. Platform is free for traders; competition accounts are an add-on.

### Cited Findings
- Prop Starter: "$3000 / per month", "Up to 500 active accounts", "Pay only for accounts that actually trade", "No setup fees or hidden costs", minimum monthly fee applies; Custom plan above 500 with "cost per account as you grow"; includes all features on web/mobile/desktop, cloud infrastructure, Back Office, reporting, CRM API, Hub listing - [TradeLocker prop firm pricing](https://tradelocker.com/prop-firm-pricing/)
- Add-ons: Competition Accounts "$290 / month for 500 accounts" (requires active prop account); Cluster Separation (dedicated server/DB), price not published - [TradeLocker prop firm pricing](https://tradelocker.com/prop-firm-pricing/)

### Inferences
- At full Starter utilisation TradeLocker costs about USD 6 per active account per month (3,000/500), but the effective floor is USD 3,000/month for a small firm.
