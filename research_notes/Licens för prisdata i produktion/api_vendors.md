# Market data API vendors for real-time spot FX and metals: price and licence for a multi-tenant white-label trading platform (October 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-06. Vendor pages were fetched live on that date (rendered in a browser where the page is JavaScript-only). "Verbatim" quotes were character-checked against the rendered page text unless marked "(summary tool)", which means the wording came from a page-summary fetch and may be slightly paraphrased. Older-than-2025 sources and secondary sources are flagged. This note builds on, and in two places corrects, research_notes/Konkurrenters priser och vårt pris/data_and_hosting_costs.md (2026-10-04).

Our use case, for reference: Ludware AB streams bid/ask for 12 symbols (EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD, NZDUSD, USDCAD, USDCHF, EURGBP, EURJPY, GBPJPY, XAUUSD, XAGUSD) at about 4 updates/s per symbol into one server, fans out to many tenants' web terminals under each tenant's brand (external display, white label, multiple client companies), fills simulated trades against the prices (non-display use), stores ticks and candles, and shows up to 30 days of chart history.

## Tiingo: does the Redistribution plan cover real-time FX and metals, what do the limits mean, is white label allowed, any per-user fee?

### Takeaway
No. The published Tiingo "Redistribution" price (USD 250/mo startups, USD 500/mo enterprise) is an "EOD + IEX REDISTRIBUTION" plan that "Includes Both End-of-Day Prices and IEX"; the identical table appears on both the Forex and the IEX product pages, so it does not cover real-time FX or metals. FX/metals display is only available "upon special request and permission" with "additional fees" (custom quote via sales@tiingo.com), and Tiingo's August 2026 terms prohibit charts/dashboards and closely-tracking resampled data (our candles) without written approval. The earlier note misread the limits: 80,000 is hourly and 1,200,000 is daily requests, the same for both price points.

### Cited Findings
- Forex product page plan table: FREE / POWER / COMMERCIAL are all "Internal Use Only" (USD 0 / 30 / 50 per month; hourly 50 / 10,000 / 20,000; daily 1,000 / 100,000 / 150,000; bandwidth 1 GB / 40 GB / 100 GB). The fourth column is titled "EOD + IEX REDISTRIBUTION", Entity "Business", License "Display Redistribution", then "80,000", "1,200,000", "1 TB", "$250/month for startups / $500/month for enterprise", "Includes Both End-of-Day Prices and IEX", "Contact sales to get started" - [Tiingo Forex API](https://www.tiingo.com/products/forex-api)

- Column alignment checked in the page DOM: the row labels are Entity, License, Usage Limits (blank), Hourly Requests, Daily Requests, Monthly Bandwidth, Plan Price, and the redistribution column reads Business / Display Redistribution / (blank) / 80,000 / 1,200,000 / 1 TB / price. ### Inferences
- For our use case Tiingo is a custom-quote vendor. The USD 250/500 figures should no longer be used as the FX display price in our cost model.
- Our stored candles and 30-day charts shown to traders fall squarely into Tiingo's prohibited "Derived Products" examples (charts that display the data; resampled data that closely tracks it). Any Tiingo FX contract must explicitly grant chart/history display and retention.
- Simulated fills (non-display) are not addressed separately; under the standard terms all use is "internal consumption only", and providing a service to prop firms is arguably not internal. Ask for explicit non-display rights.
- White label conflicts with the default attribution rule ("Data sourced by Tiingo"); a waiver must be written into the contract.
- The data itself fits: all 12 symbols are covered (XAU and XAG explicitly), bid/ask top of book over WebSocket, and thresholdLevel lets us thin the stream.

### Gaps
- Whether WebSocket traffic counts against the request or bandwidth limits is not documented.
- No published price for FX/metals redistribution; must be quoted by sales@tiingo.com.
- Tiingo's FX upstream "Forex data provider" is not named.

## Twelve Data: Venture vs Enterprise vs Enterprise+, WebSocket credits, does a multi-tenant platform need Enterprise+, any Enterprise+ price?

### Takeaway
Venture (USD 149, 299 or 499/mo depending on credits; USD 124, 248 or 414/mo billed annually) gives "External display data access"; Enterprise (from USD 1,099/mo, USD 916/mo annual) adds "External distribution market data"; only Enterprise+ (custom, no published price or range) has "White-labeling data access", and the terms put white-label and redistribution rights "Subject to separate agreement". Public display also requires the "Data provided by Twelve Data" attribution unless a white-label licence waives it, so a multi-tenant platform shown under each prop firm's brand almost certainly needs Enterprise+ or a written add-on. 12 symbols cost only 12 WebSocket credits, but Twelve Data's own Forex API v2 article says the WebSocket delivers "Mid-price updates every minute", which conflicts with the docs' bid/ask example and would not meet our 4 updates/s need; this must be tested.

### Inferences
- Tier mapping for us: Venture covers "external display" (traders see prices). Showing the data to multiple client companies (prop firms) and through their portals looks like "distribution" (Enterprise). Showing it under the prop firm's brand without Twelve Data attribution is "white-labeling" (Enterprise+). The defensible reading is that we need Enterprise+ (custom) or, at minimum, Enterprise plus a written white-label/attribution waiver.
- If tenants accept a visible "Data provided by Twelve Data" line in every terminal, Enterprise (USD 916-1,099/mo) may be enough, but only if Twelve Data confirms in writing that multi-tenant provision to prop firms is "external distribution" and not a reseller arrangement (17.1(f) "Resell access").
- Credits are not a constraint: 12 symbols = 12 WS credits; Venture's smallest tier has 500.
- Data quality is the bigger risk: if the v2 statement ("Mid-price updates every minute") applies to the WebSocket, Twelve Data cannot drive bid/ask fills or a live terminal. A trial on Venture (or the Basic trial symbols) should measure bid/ask presence and update rate for EUR/USD and XAU/USD before any contract.
- 24/7 quoting "with no interruptions for holidays or weekends" means weekend prices are synthetic/indicative; the platform must apply its own market-hours rules.
- Candles we build are "Derived Data" only if they "cannot be reverse-engineered"; OHLC candles closely reflect the source, so retention of 30-day history should be agreed explicitly (16.1 "For duration permitted by subscription").

### Gaps
- No published Enterprise+ price or range (only "Custom" and "effective rate trends well below $0.10 per credit/min").
- The "Redistribution Rights Add-On" referenced in the terms has no public catalogue or price (the add-ons page requires login).
- Whether Venture's "External display" covers end users who are customers of our customers (prop firm traders) is not defined anywhere.

- Questions to send every shortlisted vendor in writing: (1) May we display real-time bid/ask to end users of several unaffiliated client companies, inside a terminal branded by each client, without vendor attribution? (2) Is there any per-end-user, per-tenant or per-client-company fee or cap? (3) May we use the data non-display to fill simulated trades, and is that a "derivative financial product"? (4) May we store ticks and derived candles and show at least 30 days of history, and what happens to stored data on termination? (5) What is the actual WebSocket update rate and are bid and ask always present for all 12 symbols including XAGUSD?
- Cost-model impact versus the earlier note: the Tiingo USD 250/500 line should be removed (FX not included), and the realistic white-label floor from published prices is Twelve Data Enterprise at about USD 916-1,099/mo, unless Live-Rates or SiftingIO confirm that their cheaper plans cover multi-tenant white label.

- Actual update rates and bid/ask availability were not measured; a short trial on 2-3 shortlisted feeds is needed.
