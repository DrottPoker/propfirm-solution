# Prop firm CRM / challenge providers and turnkey revenue-share packages: pricing as of October 2026

Research date: 2026-10-04. Every price carries its source date. "OLD" = price older than 12 months (before Oct 2025). "Official" = vendor's own page or press release. "Third-party" = review site, blog, directory or search-engine snippet. Where a figure came only from a search-engine snippet and the underlying page was not opened, it is marked "(snippet)".

## 1. Current prices (2025-2026) for prop CRM / challenge providers that run on other vendors' trading platforms

### Takeaway
Only a handful of B2B providers publish prices: FXPropTech (full table, USD 1,000-5,000/month plus USD 1,500-6,000 setup), Execurve PropScale (EUR 740/month for 500 accounts), Propriotec (USD 3,000/month for 500 accounts, third-party figure), B2Prop (USD 1,000-3,000/month, but only a 2024 source), and Match-Trader (USD 4,000/month platform + prop CRM, 2024 source). FPFX, Axcera, Kenmore, YourPropFirm Enterprise, Trade Tech Solutions, TradeCore, Soft-FX and EAERA remain quote-only in 2026. Most CRMs need a separately licensed trading platform, and the cheapest public platform prices are about USD 2,500-3,000/month, so a small firm on a "USD 1,000/month CRM" realistically pays USD 3,500-5,500/month in total.

## 2. Turnkey revenue-share programs: what they charge now and what founders say

### Takeaway
Revenue-share turnkeys charge USD 2,000-3,000 setup and then take 50-70% of gross sales (or 50% of net), usually while also covering payouts, payment processing and the trading platform. New since our notes: Match-Prop launched 15 Sept 2026, YourPropFirm/PropSuite offers USD 2,749 setup + 50% net profit + USD 5 per sale + 5% of sales, and StartPropFirm.Today now also sells flat-fee white labels (USD 3,000-4,000/month, no revenue share). Almost no founder comments on these programs were found.

## 5. What founders report actually paying for the full stack (platform + CRM + risk + KYC + payments)

### Takeaway
No first-hand founder figures (Reddit, Forex Factory, YouTube) turned up. The available numbers come from vendors or affiliated reviewers. They put a small white-label stack at USD 2,500-5,000/month for platform + CRM, plus USD 1,500-6,000 for CRM/risk tools, KYC at about USD 1-5 per check, and payment processing at 2.5-8% of sales.

### Cited Findings
- Monthly recurring (2026, author's synthesis, not vendor-verified): platform monthly fee USD 0-2,500; trading platform access USD 0-2,000; CRM/dashboards/risk tools USD 1,500-6,000; hosting USD 200-1,000; support tooling USD 50-500; payment processing 2.5-6% per transaction; total launch lean USD 8,000-15,000, "serious" USD 30,000-70,000 - [Alex Firdaus, 2026](https://alexfirdaus.com/start-a-prop-firm/)
- Six-layer stack (Track360, 2026-05-27): trading platform USD 30K-100K setup + licensing; risk engine USD 20K-150K; KYC/AML USD 0.50-5 per check + base fee; payments 2.5-4% per transaction; CRM USD 10K-80K setup + per-seat; affiliate USD 15K-50K/year - [Track360 buyer guide](https://track360.io/blog/prop-firm-software-buyer-guide-2026)
- White-label all-in-one USD 2,000-5,000/month; "$5k+/month" if non-technical and launching in 2-4 weeks (snippet) - [DEV Community](https://dev.to/propfirmhunters/how-to-build-a-prop-trading-firm-a-beginners-guide-374j)
- White-label provider USD 8,000-20,000 vs DIY USD 250,000-500,000; total launch USD 50,000-250,000 (vendor blog, 2026-08-26) - [Trade Tech Solutions](https://www.tradetechsolutions.io/insights/how-much-does-it-cost-to-start-a-prop-firm)
- Owned stack becomes cheaper over 3 years at about 5,000-15,000 active accounts; full custom platform USD 500,000-1.2M; running cost at scale USD 25,000-60,000/month - [Plus8Soft, 2026-09-10](https://plus8soft.com/blog/prop-firm-technology-stack/)
- KYC: Sumsub Basic USD 1.35 per verification with USD 149/month minimum; Compliance USD 1.85 with USD 299 minimum (third-party, verified June 2026) - [CostBench Sumsub](https://costbench.com/software/kyc-aml/sumsub/)
- Platform access risk: about 100 prop firms closed between early 2024 and late 2025 after MetaQuotes' restrictions; cTrader restricted US onboarding in 2026 - [Finance Magnates, 2026-07-01](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/ctrader-restricts-us-prop-firm-access-following-internal-regulatory-assessment/)

### Inferences
- Indicative full-stack monthly cost for a small forex/CFD prop firm on a typical white label. Assumptions: 100 accounts = 100 KYC checks and USD 15,000 sales/month; 500 accounts = 500 checks and USD 75,000 sales.
  - 100 accounts: CRM + platform USD 3,000-5,500 + risk tool USD 0-1,400 + KYC ~USD 150 + payments 3-8% of USD 15,000 (USD 450-1,200) = **about USD 3,600-8,250/month**.
  - 500 accounts: CRM + platform USD 3,000-5,500 + risk USD 0-1,700 + KYC ~USD 675 + payments 3-8% of USD 75,000 (USD 2,250-6,000) = **about USD 5,900-13,900/month**.
- On our product the same firm would pay USD 500 (100 slots) or USD 2,500 (500 slots) for platform + CRM, plus its own KYC and Stripe fees. The saving is mostly the platform licence and the CRM fee.
- All total-stack sources are vendor content marketing or affiliated reviewers (Track360, Trade Tech Solutions and Plus8Soft are vendors; Alex Firdaus reviews for FundedTrading, which earns referral fees). Read the ranges as indicative, not as reported spend.

### Gaps
- No first-hand founder statements of real total spend were found on Reddit, Forex Factory, LinkedIn or YouTube in this search pass (searches returned only vendor blogs).
- No reported or negotiated prices (as opposed to list prices) were found for any provider.
