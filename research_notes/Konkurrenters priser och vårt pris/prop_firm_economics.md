# Unit economics of small and new forex/CFD prop firms (2025-2026)

*Research date: 2026-10-04. Source labels used below: [OFFICIAL] = firm's own disclosure or audited/registry filing; [3P DATA] = third-party dataset (tech vendor, tracker) with stated sample; [ESTIMATE] = analyst or vendor estimate without published method; [ILLUSTRATIVE] = worked example, not real data; [ANECDOTE] = single-firm story; [SEARCH SUMMARY] = figure seen only in a search-engine summary and not verified on the fetched page. Many "industry" pages are written by vendors (Track360, Spotware, FirmForge, PropAccount) who sell to prop firms, so treat their numbers as marketing-adjacent. All ranges use plain dashes.*

## Q1. Challenge fees by account size (one-step vs two-step) and median fee per challenge sold

### Takeaway
List prices at the big CFD firms in 2026 run roughly USD 30-70 for 5K-10K, 170-270 for 25K, 290-370 for 50K, 400-580 for 100K and 800-1,160 for 200K; one-step is priced about the same or slightly lower than two-step at FTMO. After discounts, the realized average fee per challenge sold is about USD 190-250 (Track360 AOV USD 187 for two-step), and the median sale sits in the 25K-50K band, so a small firm should plan on roughly USD 100-200 realized revenue per challenge sold.

### Cited Findings
- FTMO 2-Step list prices (Apr 2026): 10K EUR 89-155 (~USD 95-165), 25K EUR 199-250 (~USD 215-270), 50K EUR 345 (~USD 370), 100K EUR 540 (~USD 580), 200K EUR 1,080 (~USD 1,160); fee refunded with first profit split. [OFFICIAL list prices via comparison site] - [CompleteTradersEdge, 2026-04-28](https://completetradersedge.com/ftmo-vs-fundednext-vs-fundingpips/)
- FTMO 1-Step list prices (2026): 10K EUR 79, 25K EUR 199, 50K EUR 319, 100K EUR 499, 200K EUR 999; fee non-refundable, fixed 90% split. [SEARCH SUMMARY, not verified on page] - [PropTradingVibes FTMO pricing](https://proptradingvibes.com/blog/ftmo-account-sizes-pricing)

- Average fee per challenge, three conflicting Track360 figures: USD 187 AOV for two-step (Jul 2026) - [Track360 Affiliate Report](https://track360.io/blog/prop-firm-affiliate-economics-report-2026); ~USD 250 average (Jul 2026) - [Track360 Statistics 2026](https://www.track360.io/blog/prop-trading-industry-statistics-2026); USD 99 average and USD 49 average reset (May 2026) - [Track360 Operator Economics](https://track360.io/blog/how-do-prop-firms-make-money-operator-economics-guide)
- Per trader spend: average challenge spend per account over its activity cycle USD 800, ~3 challenges per account (FPFX Tech, 300,000+ accounts, 10 firms, published Sep 2024, OLDER). [3P DATA] - [Finance Magnates, 2024-09-18](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/analysis/exclusive-only-7-of-300000-prop-trading-accounts-achieved-payouts/); later update says average challenge investment fell from USD 800 to USD 400. - [FM Data Lab, dated 2025-06-20](https://datalab.financemagnates.com/market-insights/100-prop-firms-fold-but-one-buys-major-fx-broker/)
- Repeat buying: 2.6 challenge purchases per buyer per year; repeats = 55-65% of challenge revenue; buyer LTV USD 430-490 over 24 months (~2.4x first purchase). - Challenge fees range USD 99-999; instant funding costs 2-3x an evaluation fee (data to Feb 2026). - [PropTradingVibes Q1 2026](https://proptradingvibes.com/state-of-prop-trading/q1-2026)
- New-firm example: "USD 100-500 for account sizes of USD 10,000-200,000" [ILLUSTRATIVE] - [Spencer Logic, 2026-06-11](https://www.spencerlogic.com/blog/how-to-start-prop-trading-firm/)

### Inferences
- Weighting Track360's size mix by mid-band list prices (USD 69 / 200 / 315 / 525 / 1,050) gives an average list price of ~USD 330 per challenge; Track360's realized AOV of USD 187-250 implies typical discounting of roughly 25-45%, consistent with the heavy coupon culture described in the affiliate report.
- Median challenge sold: with Track360's mix, the 50th percentile falls in the 50K bucket (cumulative 46% at 25K); with the Swiset mix (10K most common) it falls lower. A reasonable 2026 median for list price is USD 170-320 and for realized revenue USD 120-250 at big firms.
- Small and new firms typically undercut FTMO by 20-50% (FundingPips-style pricing) and discount heavily to get first sales, so USD 100-200 realized per challenge sold is a prudent planning number for our customers; USD 150 is a reasonable central case.
- One-step vs two-step: at FTMO the one-step is 8-11% cheaper per size; at most firms the fee difference is small, but one-step accounts end faster (one phase), which matters for slot occupancy (see Q3).

### Gaps
- No firm publishes its median or average fee per challenge sold; all averages are vendor estimates and they conflict (USD 99 vs 187 vs 250).
- No systematic price data for small/new firms (under 1,000 sales per month); the price range comes from comparison of big brands.
- Share of one-step vs two-step sales is not quantified beyond "one-phase dominates" (Swiset).

## Q2. Pass rates for phase 1 and phase 2, and share of funded traders who get a payout

### Takeaway
Per account sold, roughly 22-35% pass phase 1, 27-45% of those pass phase 2, giving 6-14% funded (one-step ~20%, two-step ~12% in Swiset data), and 20-45% of funded traders get at least one payout, so only ~3-8% of all accounts sold ever produce a payout. Per trader (with retries) the funded rate is far higher (Topstep: 51.8% of traders funded at least once).

### Cited Findings
- FPFX Tech study (300,000+ accounts, 100,000 traders, 10 firms; published Sep 2024, OLDER): 14% pass a challenge; 45% of funded account holders get a payout; 7% of all traders reach a payout; average payout 4% of allocated capital. [3P DATA] - [Finance Magnates, 2024-09-18](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/analysis/exclusive-only-7-of-300000-prop-trading-accounts-achieved-payouts/)
- Update of the same series: pass rate 14% -> 12%, payout achievement 7% -> 8%. [3P DATA] - [FM Data Lab, dated 2025-06-20](https://datalab.financemagnates.com/market-insights/100-prop-firms-fold-but-one-buys-major-fx-broker/)
- Swiset (10,000+ traders, Apr 2024-Apr 2025): one-step pass 20.3%, two-step 11.8%. [3P DATA] - [Fortunly](https://fortunly.com/statistics/prop-firm-challenge-pass-rate-statistics/); [TradeInformer/Propinder: one-phase ~80% fail, two-phase ~90% fail, 2026-05-08](https://www.tradeinformer.com/prop-weekly/propinder-prop-traders-average-4-day-pass-time-10k-accounts-most-popular-globally)
- ATFunded (broker-backed new firm launched Oct 2024; June 2025 data, published 2025-07-02): phase 1 -> phase 2 22.6%, phase 2 -> funded 26.9%, overall funded 6%. [OFFICIAL] - [Finance Magnates via TradingView](https://vn.tradingview.com/news/financemagnates:870fcc160094b:0-prop-firm-atfunded-reports-6-of-traders-reached-funded-status-in-june-trading-data)
- Track360 (2025-2026 window): phase 1 25-35%, phase 2 30-45% of phase-1 passers, blended challenge-to-funded 12.3%, ~45% of funded get a payout, ~7% end to end, 1-3% become consistently paid long-term traders. [3P DATA/ESTIMATE] - [Track360 Statistics 2026, Jul 2026](https://www.track360.io/blog/prop-trading-industry-statistics-2026)
- FundedNext: ~24.8% advance from phase 1 to phase 2 and ~43.2% pass phase 2. [SEARCH SUMMARY, original disclosure not located] - [Track360 Statistics 2026](https://www.track360.io/blog/prop-trading-industry-statistics-2026). FundedNext average ~9%, one-step 11-12%, two-step 6-9%. [SEARCH SUMMARY, unverified] - [CoinLaw FundedNext statistics](https://coinlaw.io/fundednext-statistics/)
- FundedNext Feb 2026 payout report [OFFICIAL]: USD 15.19M paid to 8,340 traders on 10,346 funded accounts, 13,712 transactions; median payout USD 567, mean USD 1,119; 50% of paid accounts had prior payouts; lifetime payouts USD 271.4M. - [Finance Magnates, 2026-03-06](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/analysis/prop-firm-fundednext-says-it-paid-1519m-to-8340-traders-in-february/)
- Fintokei (2024) [OFFICIAL]: phase 1 -> 2 15-20%, phase 2 -> funded 15-25%, funded -> payout ~40%. The Funded Trader (2025) [OFFICIAL]: 5% challenge pass, 20% funded-to-payout. E8 Markets [OFFICIAL]: 17.7% pass. - [Fortunly compilation](https://fortunly.com/statistics/prop-firm-challenge-pass-rate-statistics/)
- Futures comparison, Topstep 2025 [OFFICIAL as reported]: 16.8% of Trading Combines passed; 51.8% of individual traders reached funded at least once; 33.3% of Express Funded traders received a payout; 0.71% of starters reached a Live Funded Account. - [Tradecovex, updated Apr 2026](https://tradecovex.com/guides/how-many-combines-passed-express-topstep-2025). Other futures: Earn2Trade 8.89% (2025), Take Profit Trader 16.86% (2024), TradeDay 28.2%. - [Fortunly](https://fortunly.com/statistics/prop-firm-challenge-pass-rate-statistics/)
- FTMO does not publish an official phase-by-phase pass rate; estimates are ~10-15%. [SEARCH SUMMARY] - [JP Trading Capital](https://www.jptradingcapital.com/blog/en/ftmo-challenge-pass-rate-statistics). ThePropFirmGuide claims FTMO's standard 2-Step is in the "9-10%" range and labels this an official disclosure (not verified). - [ThePropFirmGuide](https://thepropfirmguide.com/prop-firm-statistics/)
- Benchmarks used in a 2026 startup guide: 2-8% pass for two-step, 6-15% for one-step. [ESTIMATE] - [Alex Firdaus, 2026](https://alexfirdaus.com/start-a-prop-firm/); "7-12% across two-step evaluations" - [Track360, 2026-07-18](https://track360.io/blog/prop-firm-affiliate-economics-report-2026)
- Failure modes: 71% of phase-1 failures are daily drawdown breaches - [HOC Trade, 2026-07-02](https://hoc-trade.com/blogs/trading-psychology/prop-firm-challenge-why-fail); OneFunded: 78.7% of failed evaluations breached daily loss, 15% max drawdown [OFFICIAL] - [Fortunly](https://fortunly.com/statistics/prop-firm-challenge-pass-rate-statistics/); 60-70% of failures end on drawdown violation rather than expiry - [Track360 Statistics](https://www.track360.io/blog/prop-trading-industry-statistics-2026); time-limit expiry only 5-10% of failures - [ThePropFirmGuide](https://thepropfirmguide.com/prop-firm-statistics/)

### Inferences
- Funnel per 100 two-step accounts sold (central case): ~28 reach phase 2, ~10 funded, ~4 get a payout. One-step: ~20 funded, ~5-9 paid.
- Pass rates are a design choice for the firm (rules); a new firm with looser rules sees higher pass rates and higher payout exposure (Alex Firdaus: 12% vs 25% pass changes exposure from 20% to 83% of revenue, see Q6).
- Per-trader vs per-account matters for us: the slot model is per account, so per-account rates (6-14% funded) are the relevant numbers.

### Gaps
- FTMO, The5ers and FundingPips do not publish phase-by-phase pass rates; FundedNext's phase figures could not be traced to an original disclosure.
- No dataset specific to small firms; all large samples come from established firms or vendor platforms.

## Q3. How long a challenge account stays active (slot occupancy)

### Takeaway
Most failing accounts breach within the first week (often in the first few sessions), successful traders pass fast (average 4-6 days in the Swiset data), but a tail of "grinders" and idle accounts can stay open for weeks, and funded accounts live much longer (FundedNext: 14% of paid accounts active 90+ days). A reasonable central estimate is ~2-4 weeks of slot occupancy per challenge sold when phase 2 and funded time are included; no firm publishes average days-to-breach.

### Cited Findings
- Swiset (10,000+ traders, Apr 2024-Apr 2025): successful traders reach funded status in ~6 days on average; Africa and Central America ~3 days, Caribbean ~14 days. [3P DATA] - [Fortunly](https://fortunly.com/statistics/prop-firm-challenge-pass-rate-statistics/); [Finance Magnates via TradingView](https://tr.tradingview.com/news/financemagnates%3Ab64d2ea39094b%3A0-80-fail-in-prop-trading-globally-south-american-traders-aim-to-reverse-the-trend)
- Same dataset as reported in 2026: average pass time "under four days"; traders who fail "can spend weeks trying to grind it out before blowing the account". [3P DATA] - [TradeInformer on Propinder, 2026-05-08](https://www.tradeinformer.com/prop-weekly/propinder-prop-traders-average-4-day-pass-time-10k-accounts-most-popular-globally)
- Most failures happen "in the first week, on a loss-limit breach", before the trader nears the profit target; no median days figure given. - [HOC Trade, 2026-07-02](https://hoc-trade.com/blogs/trading-psychology/prop-firm-challenge-why-fail); also [ThePropFirmGuide](https://thepropfirmguide.com/prop-firm-statistics/)
- Counter-view from trader-education content: passers take 30-90 days; one-step 15-45 days, two-step 30-90 days (2026). [ESTIMATE, SEARCH SUMMARY] - [JoinProp](https://joinprop.com/academy/how-long-it-really-takes-to-pass-a-prop-firm-challenge/); contradicted by Swiset's 4-6 day average.
- FundedNext Feb 2026 [OFFICIAL]: 60% (of paid accounts) funded within 30 days; 14% active 90+ days; 415 accounts live 6+ months. - [Finance Magnates, 2026-03-06](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/analysis/prop-firm-fundednext-says-it-paid-1519m-to-8340-traders-in-february/)
- Topstep: only 33.3% of Express Funded traders ever get a payout, i.e. most funded accounts also end by breach. [OFFICIAL as reported] - [Tradecovex](https://tradecovex.com/guides/how-many-combines-passed-express-topstep-2025)
- Failure by time-limit expiry is only 5-10% of failures, i.e. most challenges end by breach rather than by deadline. - [ThePropFirmGuide](https://thepropfirmguide.com/prop-firm-statistics/)
- Re-purchase timing: 40-60% of failed traders reset within 30-60 days; 30-40% of failed buyers repurchase within 90 days. [ESTIMATE] - [Track360 Operator Economics](https://track360.io/blog/how-do-prop-firms-make-money-operator-economics-guide); [Track360 Statistics](https://www.track360.io/blog/prop-trading-industry-statistics-2026)
- Launch timeline example: first payouts come weeks 8-12 after launch, i.e. funded accounts appear ~1-2 months after the first sales. - [FirmForge, 2026-08-01](https://www.firmforge.cfd/how-to-start-a-prop-firm)

### Inferences
- Slot-days per challenge sold (two-step), built from the cited funnel and timing data:
  - Low: phase 1 avg 7 days + 25% x 7 days phase 2 + 6% x 30 days funded = ~11 days (~0.35 slot-months).
  - Central: 12 days + 30% x 10 days + 10% x 60 days = ~21 days (~0.7 slot-months).
  - High: 20 days + 35% x 14 days + 14% x 90 days = ~38 days (~1.25 slot-months).
  - One-step challenges occupy fewer slot-days (no phase 2) but fund more accounts (~20%), so funded time dominates.
- By Little's law, concurrent active accounts = challenges sold per month x slot-months per challenge, so a firm selling N challenges/month needs roughly 0.35N-1.25N slots, central ~0.7N.
- The biggest uncertainty is idle accounts: most 2026 challenges have no time limit, so accounts that stop trading (without breaching) would hold a slot indefinitely unless the firm enforces an inactivity rule. Our product should auto-release slots on inactivity (for example 30 days without a trade) or count only accounts that traded in the period (TradeLocker bills "accounts that actually trade", see Q8).
- Funded accounts are the long-lived slots but generate no fee revenue; a funded slot at USD 5/month is trivial versus the median payout of USD 567 (FundedNext).

### Gaps
- No firm or vendor publishes mean or median days-to-breach; the "first week" finding is qualitative. A widely repeated "82% fail within the first week" figure appeared only in a search summary and could not be traced to a source.
- No data on the share of accounts that go idle without breaching, or on typical inactivity rules across firms.
- No data on average funded-account lifetime beyond FundedNext's 14% active 90+ days.

## Q4. Active accounts and challenges sold per month at a small or new firm

### Takeaway
There is no hard disclosed data for small firms; the available numbers are illustrative examples (50-500 sales/month, ~200 active challenges a month after launch) and anecdotes, including a firm that sold 4 challenges in its first week and closed. Vendor tiers (TradeLocker's starter tier is "up to 500 active accounts") suggest small firms run from tens to a few hundred concurrent accounts.

### Cited Findings
- "Within a month of launch" a small founder can have ~200 traders running challenges; worked example of 200 challenge sales/month at USD 300 average = USD 60,000/month. [ILLUSTRATIVE] - [Alex Firdaus, 2026](https://alexfirdaus.com/start-a-prop-firm/)
- Illustrative mid-scale firm: 2,000 active challenge accounts at USD 150 per cycle = USD 300,000/month challenge fees; example first-year target USD 500K challenge revenue. [ILLUSTRATIVE] - [Spencer Logic, 2026-06-11](https://www.spencerlogic.com/blog/how-to-start-prop-trading-firm/)
- Ascetic Capital launched 2024-11-28, sold four challenges in its first week, investors pulled out, closed 2024-12-03 and refunded customers. [ANECDOTE] - [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/prop-firm-shuts-down-after-one-week-we-only-made-four-sales/)
- PropAccount white-label example: USD 20,000 monthly challenge sales, founder keeps 30% (USD 6,000/month). [ILLUSTRATIVE] - [Alex Firdaus, 2026](https://alexfirdaus.com/start-a-prop-firm/)
- Breakeven claim: below ~2,000-3,000 monthly evaluation sales most prop firms run at break-even or a loss (assumes ~USD 150K/month operations, CAC USD 30-100). [ESTIMATE, vendor] - [Track360 Operator Economics, 2026-05-28](https://track360.io/blog/how-do-prop-firms-make-money-operator-economics-guide)
- Break-even in 4-8 months "assuming a solid trader funnel". [ESTIMATE, vendor] - [Spotware, 2026-01-08](https://www.spotware.com/news/how-to-start-a-proprietary-trading-firm-2026/)
- Vendor sizing: TradeLocker Starter plan USD 3,000/month for up to 500 active (actually trading) accounts; custom above 500. - [TradeLocker prop pricing, accessed 2026-10](https://tradelocker.com/prop-firm-pricing/)
- Large-firm scale for contrast: FundedNext paid 10,346 funded accounts in Feb 2026 [OFFICIAL] - [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/analysis/prop-firm-fundednext-says-it-paid-1519m-to-8340-traders-in-february/); Track360 estimates FTMO at 380-450K active traders and USD 180-210M revenue [ESTIMATE, conflicts with FTMO's audited USD 329M for 2024] - [Track360 Statistics](https://www.track360.io/blog/prop-trading-industry-statistics-2026)
- New traders avoid new firms: trust and payout history is cited as the main barrier, and a firm's first payouts come 8-12 weeks after launch. - [FirmForge, 2026-08-01](https://www.firmforge.cfd/how-to-start-a-prop-firm)

### Inferences
- Using the Q3 central ratio (0.7 slot-months per challenge sold): a new firm selling 50 challenges/month needs ~35 slots; 200/month needs ~140; 500/month needs ~350; 2,000/month ~1,400.
- A plausible first-year path for a small white-label firm: 20-100 sales/month in months 1-3, 100-500 by month 6-12 if marketing works, which maps to roughly 15-70 concurrent slots early and 70-350 later. Many firms never pass the low end (Ascetic Capital).
- Monthly revenue of a small firm therefore ranges from ~USD 3-15K (50-100 sales at USD 60-150) to ~USD 50-100K (500 sales at USD 100-200).

### Gaps
- No small firm publishes monthly sales or active-account counts; no Reddit or Forex Factory founder thread with verifiable numbers was found in this pass.
- No vendor publishes the distribution of its customers by active accounts.

## Q5. Cost to start a prop firm in 2026 and the technology share of the budget

### Takeaway
Guides put a lean white-label launch at USD 8-15K and a "survival-ready" launch at USD 30-70K, with vendor-written guides citing USD 50-200K; technology is ~25-40% of a lean budget but only ~10-20% of a well-funded one, and founders and advisors consistently name payout reserves, high-risk payment processing and traffic (not technology) as the biggest obstacles.

### Cited Findings
- Lean launch USD 8,000-15,000; survival-ready USD 30,000-70,000; the difference is payout reserves and legal, not technology. Line items (lean / serious): white-label platform USD 3-5K / 8-20K; monthly platform fee USD 0-500 / 500-2,500; trading platform access USD 0 if bundled / 500-2,000 per month; SVG entity USD 500-1,500; Dubai DMCC USD 9-16K first year; legal documents USD 500-1,500 / 2-5K; brand/website USD 1-3K / 4-10K; marketing USD 2-5K for 90 days / 5-15K per month; payout reserve USD 5-10K / 15-40K+. - [Alex Firdaus, 2026](https://alexfirdaus.com/start-a-prop-firm/)
- Monthly running costs (same guide): CRM/dashboards USD 1,500-6,000; trading platform licence USD 400-3,000; hosting USD 200-1,000; KYC USD 1-4 per user; support tools USD 50-500; card processing 2.5-6%. - [Alex Firdaus, 2026](https://alexfirdaus.com/start-a-prop-firm/)
- Total USD 50,000-200,000: platform licensing USD 8-25K upfront, tech stack USD 3-8K/month, risk/liquidity USD 5-50K+, legal USD 3-10K, marketing USD 5-15K, working capital USD 20-100K+; white-label setup ~1 week vs 2-4 months for own label. [vendor] - [Spotware, 2026-01-08](https://www.spotware.com/news/how-to-start-a-proprietary-trading-firm-2026/)
- Launch estimate USD 5,000-65,000: platform setup USD 3-50K, platform fees USD 2-15K/month, trading platform and data USD 1-10K/month; custom build USD 50-150K and 9-18 months. [vendor] - [FirmForge, 2026-08-01](https://www.firmforge.cfd/how-to-start-a-prop-firm)
- White-label setup USD 5-50K, SaaS fee USD 2-15K/month (often tiered by active traders), trading platform/data USD 1-10K/month, CRM/support/compliance USD 1-8K/month. [vendor] - [Track360, 2026-05-31](https://track360.io/blog/white-label-prop-firm-cost-providers-setup-2026)
- Challenge-only operation USD 30,000-100,000; live-funded programs USD 250,000-1,000,000 of accessible capital. [ILLUSTRATIVE] - [Spencer Logic, 2026-06-11](https://www.spencerlogic.com/blog/how-to-start-prop-trading-firm/)
- Payout reserve is "the single most common cause of early-stage firm death"; a firm can need USD 80-120K in payout reserves within days. [SEARCH SUMMARY] - [TradeTech Solutions](https://www.tradetechsolutions.io/insights/how-much-does-it-cost-to-start-a-prop-firm); a firm doing USD 1M/month can owe 30-50% of revenue to funded traders within 90 days - [FirmForge](https://www.firmforge.cfd/how-to-start-a-prop-firm)
- Founder obstacles ranked: (1) underestimating payout reserves, (2) launching on mainstream processors without backup ("Stripe, PayPal routinely freeze or terminate prop firm accounts"), (3) copying competitor rules without the math, (4) scaling before operations stabilize, (5) ignoring marketing regulations (UK FCA). - Payment processor approval takes 14-30 days. - [Track360, 2026-07-18](https://track360.io/blog/prop-firm-affiliate-economics-report-2026)

### Inferences

- Our example setup fee (USD 500) is 6-10x below the cheapest cited white-label setups (USD 3-5K) and far below the USD 5-50K vendor ranges. A first-year tech bill of USD 500 + slots (for example 50-150 slots x USD 5 x 12 = USD 3-9K) is below the lean-case tech line (USD 3-5K setup + USD 0-6K/year fees) only at the low end and comparable at the high end.

### Gaps

## Q6. Share of revenue going to payouts, payment processing, affiliates/marketing and technology

### Takeaway
For a typical challenge-model firm, payouts to traders take ~20-50% of revenue (higher for loose rules), affiliates and marketing ~20-40%, payment processing 3-8% plus 2-4% refunds/chargebacks, and the firm keeps ~15-30% net (FTMO: 19% net margin on USD 329M in 2024). Technology is usually priced as USD 2-15K/month plus USD 1-10 per account or as a 30-50% revenue share for turnkey white-label deals.

### Cited Findings
- FTMO 2024 [OFFICIAL, Czech registry filings via press]: parent revenue CZK 6.84bn (~USD 329M, +53% YoY), net profit CZK 1.3bn (~USD 62.5M, ~19% net margin); "services" covering trading platform operations, payouts to clients and marketing = CZK 4.11bn (~USD 197M, ~60% of revenue) vs CZK 2.08bn (~USD 100M) in 2023. - [Finance Magnates via TradingView, 2025](https://www.tradingview.com/news/financemagnates:24b2ebd05094b:0-ftmo-s-parent-netted-over-62-million-on-329m-revenue-in-2024/)

FTMO at scale shows ~19% net.
- At revenue-share pricing (30-50%) a turnkey vendor takes more than marketing; at per-account pricing (USD 1-10 per account) tech is 1-5% of revenue at USD 150 per challenge. Our slot model sits in the per-account family.

## Q8. Synthesis: is USD 5 per slot per month plus USD 500 setup cheap, fair or too expensive?

### Takeaway
At realistic small-firm numbers (USD 100-200 realized per challenge, ~0.35-1.25 slot-months per challenge sold), USD 5 per slot per month costs the firm about USD 2-6 per challenge sold, i.e. ~1-6% of challenge revenue (central ~2.3%), which is cheap relative to cited vendor models (30-50% revenue share, or USD 2-15K/month plus USD 1-10 per account) and comparable to the low end of per-account pricing. The USD 500 setup fee is far below every cited white-label setup (USD 3-50K).

### Cited Findings
- Per-account fees in the market: USD 1-10 per account or per evaluation, on top of setup and monthly platform fees. - [Track360 White Label, 2026-05-31](https://track360.io/blog/white-label-prop-firm-cost-providers-setup-2026); [FirmForge, 2026-08-01](https://www.firmforge.cfd/how-to-start-a-prop-firm)
- TradeLocker (trading platform only): USD 3,000/month for up to 500 active accounts, billed on accounts that actually trade; no setup fee. That is USD 6 per account per month at full use and more at lower use. - [TradeLocker, accessed 2026-10](https://tradelocker.com/prop-firm-pricing/)
- Turnkey white-label: USD 3,000 setup and the founder keeps 30% of gross sales (PropAccount WL1); revenue share models 30-50%. - [Alex Firdaus](https://alexfirdaus.com/start-a-prop-firm/); [FirmForge](https://www.firmforge.cfd/how-to-start-a-prop-firm)
- Lean-launch white-label platform USD 3-5K plus USD 0-500/month; serious USD 8-20K plus USD 500-2,500/month. - [Alex Firdaus](https://alexfirdaus.com/start-a-prop-firm/)
- Payment processing alone takes 3-8% of sales and affiliates 10-15% of the fee. - [Track360 White Label](https://track360.io/blog/white-label-prop-firm-cost-providers-setup-2026); [Track360 Affiliate Report](https://track360.io/blog/prop-firm-affiliate-economics-report-2026)

### Inferences
- Cost per challenge sold at USD 5/slot-month: low occupancy 0.35 slot-months -> USD 1.75; central 0.7 -> USD 3.50; high 1.25 -> USD 6.25.
- As share of realized challenge revenue: central USD 3.50 / USD 150 = ~2.3%; best case USD 1.75 / USD 200 = ~0.9%; worst case USD 6.25 / USD 100 = ~6.3%. Add 30-50% for unused prepaid headroom if firms buy slots ahead of demand: ~1.2-9%, central ~3%.
- Revenue per slot-month for the firm: USD 150 / 0.7 = ~USD 214 (range ~USD 80-570). USD 5 is ~2.3% of that in the central case.
- Monthly examples (central assumptions): 50 sales/month -> ~35 slots -> USD 175/month vs USD 7,500 revenue; 200 sales -> ~140 slots -> USD 700 vs USD 30,000; 500 sales -> ~350 slots -> USD 1,750 vs USD 75,000 (still below TradeLocker's USD 3,000 minimum for the trading platform alone).
- Pricing headroom: payment processing (3-8%) and per-account vendor fees (USD 1-10) set the "normal" cost anchors. USD 8-10 per slot would still land at ~4-5% of revenue in the central case and stay well below revenue-share alternatives; above ~USD 15 per slot the worst case (USD 100 fee, long occupancy) would exceed 15% of revenue and start to bite. Conclusion: USD 5 is cheap-to-fair; there is room to go to ~USD 8-10, but the setup fee of USD 500 is very low versus the market and could be raised (USD 1,000-3,000 is still at or below the cheapest cited setups) if positioning allows.
- Main risk to the firm's cost is slot occupancy, not the price: idle unlimited-time challenges and long-lived funded accounts. Inactivity-based release or billing only on accounts that traded (as TradeLocker does) keeps the effective cost per challenge in the USD 2-4 range.

### Gaps
- No direct data on how often small firms over-buy capacity, or on real occupancy distributions; the slot-month model rests on pass rates and qualitative timing data.
- Vendor price lists beyond TradeLocker were not verified in this pass (competitor pricing is covered in a separate research note).
