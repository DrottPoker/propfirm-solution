# All-in-one white-label prop firm technology providers (own trading platform + challenge/prop CRM): pricing as of October 2026

Research date: 2026-10-04. "Page as fetched 2026-10-04" means the price was read live on that date and the page itself carries no price date. Currency conversions use an assumed EUR/USD of 1.17 (not sourced, verify before publishing).

Scope note: only a few vendors truly sell BOTH their own trading platform AND their own prop/challenge CRM as one priced package: Fintatech, Match-Trade (Turnkey), Leverate (SiRiX + Prop CRM), Quadcode, B2Broker (B2Prop, but on white-label cTrader), Volumetrica, Spotware cTrader (startup prop offer) and newcomer Soft-FX. TradeLocker sells the platform only (CRM via API). DXtrade relies on partners (e.g. Arizet for risk). FXPropTech, PropAccount and PropSuite are CRM/rev-share layers on third-party platforms, listed only as benchmarks.

## Q1. What are the current (2025-2026) published or reliably reported prices per provider?

### Takeaway
Only three vendors publish full list prices in 2026: Fintatech (EUR 0 to 7,000/month, tiered by active accounts, with per-account overage), TradeLocker (USD 3,000/month minimum up to 500 active accounts, platform only) and Match-Trade (USD 2,500 Basic / 4,000 Turnkey with prop CRM / 5,000 Server, "per-account basis", but included account counts are not published). cTrader, DXtrade, Volumetrica, Quadcode (monthly), ProjectX, Tradovate/NinjaTrader and Soft-FX are quote-only. Fintatech is the only vendor with a published EUR 0/month entry tier, and it is the closest list-price comparator to our model.

## Q2. How do they define and count billable accounts?

### Takeaway
Only TradeLocker publishes an exact definition (at least one open position during the billing month). Fintatech, Match-Trader, FXPropTech and cTrader's prop server bill per account but do not define "active"; Leverate counts "real accounts"; B2Prop claimed no account-based charges at all.

### Cited Findings
- TradeLocker: active account = "An account that had at least one open position during the billing month. You don't pay for inactive users."; minimum fee applies "if there are fewer than 500 accounts in a billing month" - [TradeLocker](https://tradelocker.com/prop-firm-pricing/)
- Fintatech: pricing keyed to "active accounts" ("EUR 0 / month (If <= 50 active accounts)"), but no definition on the page or in the 2026-09-21 blog - [Fintatech](https://fintatech.com/prop-firm/); [Fintatech blog](https://fintatech.com/blog/bootstrapping-your-prop-firm-how-to-launch-with-zero-tech-risk-and-0-monthly-saas-minimums/)
- Match-Trader: "pricing is structured on a per-account basis"; definition not published - [Match-Trader](https://match-trader.com/prop-trading-platform/)
- Leverate: plans count "real accounts" (500 / 1,500 / 5,000+) - [Leverate](https://leverate.com/)
- cTrader prop server: charged "per account rather than trading volumes" - [Spotware news via search](https://www.spotware.com/news/b2broker-launches-ctrader-white-label-prop-trading-solution/)
- B2Prop: "no account-based charges", unlimited user registrations (2024) - [BusinessWire via search](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240709010038/en)
- FXPropTech: limits in "accounts" with USD 2.50 overage - [FXPropTech](https://fxproptech.com/pricing.html)

### Inferences
- "Active = traded in the month" (TradeLocker) is the de facto industry reference. Since challenge accounts that never trade are free under it, it favours firms with many abandoned purchases.
- Leverate's "real accounts" may not cover simulated prop accounts at all; prop pricing could follow a different unit.

### Gaps
- No vendor found that bills on concurrent challenges or funded accounts only; no clarity on whether failed/expired challenge accounts count in the month they close.
